# Benchmark results: railway network (v0.3)

Live results on the held-out **test** seeds (10000–19999) of the v0.3 network benchmark, regenerated by
`scripts/live_scoreboard.py` while episodes arrive. Three models are compared: Gemini 4 Carbon
(`gemini-carbon-high`), Qwen 3.8 27B before post-training (`qwen38-think`) and the same Qwen after
supervised fine-tuning (`qwen38-think-sft`, also accepted as `qwen38-sft`). The fine-tuning data came
from an offline teacher on the disjoint train seeds (20000+), so no test seed was ever seen in training.

## How to read the numbers

- **Reward** is the negative total cost, −(C1+C2+C3+C4+C5); closer to zero is better. The cost parts are C1 passenger lateness; C2 unfinished trains / missed calls; C3 freight lateness; C4 crew timeouts; C5 gridlock and operations (unplanned stops, platform changes).
- Every seed counts equally: reps of a seed are averaged first, then seeds are averaged.
- The **95% CI** resamples seeds (bootstrap). **Rep std** is how much a model's reward moves between repeated runs of the same seed: the benchmark's run-to-run noise.
- **Paired** comparisons use only seeds both models finished, so a hard seed cannot favour either side.
- The **do-nothing floor** is the deterministic score of ending the shift at once on the same seeds; it shows the scale, it is not a competitor.

## Reading of the current snapshot

- Gemini 4 Carbon has finished 87 of 270 planned episodes (32%).
- Qwen 3.8 27B (base) has finished 18 of 270 planned episodes (7%).
- Qwen 3.8 27B (fine-tuned) has finished 13 of 30 planned episodes (43%).
- On T1, over 12 seeds both models finished, Carbon scores -1,955.2 and base Qwen -1,998.6 (43.4 points apart, 95% CI [-1,515.6, 1,509.6]): the difference is not yet distinguishable from noise.
- On T2, over 1 seeds both models finished, Carbon scores -1,112.8 and base Qwen -2,504.9 (1,392.1 points apart, 95% CI [1,392.1, 1,392.1]): too few paired seeds for a verdict (need 5).
- On the 7 T1 test seeds all three models finished, fine-tuning moved Qwen from -1,915.5 to -1,323.7 (+591.8, 95% CI [-1,041.8, 2,385.0]; better on 4 seeds, worse on 1), against -1,559.3 for Carbon, closing 166.1% of the gap.
