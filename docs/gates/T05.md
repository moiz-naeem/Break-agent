## Decisions and deviations

- **Engine reports movements (additive).** The engine used to emit only `AUTHORITY_CUT` changes, which left the
  advance output's "changes since" lines and the stringline history without movement data. It now emits:
  - `ENTRY`: the train appears at its origin yard;
  - `ENTER`: the train enters a section (also when reversing);
  - `ARRIVE`: the train reaches a station or a yard.

  The stringline tests (T05-15) found three places still missing these, all now fixed:
  - entries exactly at the entry tick;
  - arrivals at the destination yard;
  - reversal arrivals.

  The session seeds its history with the trains already in the network at load. `State` stays compact, as the
  ticket requires.
- **Engine bug found while tracing (fixed):** `_reverse` released the station track *before* its two
  `R-NO-REVERSE-PATH` checks, so a refused reverse was not a no-op (breaks T02-46). The release now happens after
  the checks. Regression test: `tests/t02/test_gridlock.py::test_T02_28b_refused_reverse_is_noop`, red before the
  fix and green after.
- **Engine speed (same results):** `Simulator.clone()` deep-copied the whole scenario (pydantic models) for
  every `check()`.
  - Greedy took up to 16 s per T2 episode (T05-34 needs < 3 s).
  - `clone()` now shares the scenario-derived lookups, which are never mutated after load, and copies train
    state by hand. Arrival records are append-only; authorities get a new dict and path list.
  - Costs are identical on all 10 T2 dev seeds before and after. Greedy now takes 0.76 s in-process at most.
  - T02-04 and T02-24 (clone independence and check == apply-on-clone) stay green.
- **Formats** where the PRD example and the ticket grammar differ:
  - `auth:` shows `<station>/<track>` without a loop-kind suffix (T05-08 expects `auth:S2/main`);
  - `waiting` lines have no `auth`/`delay` (PRD §7.8.1 example);
  - `advance` reasons show their detail in parentheses, e.g. `BREAKDOWN E1 (until t=14)`.
- **Stringline:**
  - The rows are `[ >]t<tick>` in a 6-character label, then 4-character cells.
  - Rows at or before the current tick come from history only. Later rows add a projection of current
    authorities from `Simulator.from_observation`, so no hidden event can show.
  - T05-16's pinned breakdown example kills the mutant that projects from the true simulator.
- **MCP server:**
  - Only arguments that differ from their default are forwarded to `Session.call`. So `status()` and
    `status(compact=false)` both log `{}`, exactly as an in-process call that omits the argument (needed for
    log parity, T05-18 and T06-13).
  - The rules-version check reads the raw JSON *before* model validation, which would otherwise echo the
    scenario on stderr and exit 1.
  - On Windows, `spawn` kills the whole process tree. The venv `python.exe` is a launcher, so `terminate()`
    left the real server running.
- **Runner over MCP:** the final result comes from `replay(scenario, episode_log)`, so the counts are recomputed
  exactly as the verifier will. The runner also checks that it equals the live `final_cost`.

## Calibration (approved by the user: "Recalibrate")

With DESIGN's T1/T2 sizes, greedy could not finish every train on **any** dev seed, and it used up to 389 of 400
calls on T2. T05-35 and T06's seed filter (which needs "greedy finishes every train") could not pass. Measured on
10 dev seeds each, then changed:

| What | DESIGN / before | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 `shift_ticks` | 120 | 240 | 8 trains entering in the first ~40 ticks could not cross on single track: greedy finished 0/20 seeds; at 240, 14/20 |
| T1/T2 `end_margin` (new `GenConfig` field, default 15) | 15 | 60 / 120 | late entries need room for typical delays before the shift ends |
| T2 trains / `shift_ticks` | 14 / 360 | 10 / 720 | at 14/360 greedy finished 0/10 even with unlimited budget; 10/720 finishes most |
| T2 `event_density` | 1.0 | 0.5 | events scale with shift length; this keeps decision points ≤ 120 |
| `TOOL_BUDGET` | 400 | 600 | greedy needs up to 370 calls on T2; T05-35 needs < 70% |
| Generator: 700 m loops | one per third of the line | also never 3 stations in a row without one | freight (600 m) starved: the T06 reference only sends a train where a fitting loop lies within 3 stations beyond |

T2 is still 12 stations. `docs/PRD.md` (`TOOL_BUDGET`) and `tests/t04/test_generator.py`/`test_cli.py` (preset
pins) are updated, with comments pointing here.

**Calibration numbers (T05-32..35, 10 T2 dev seeds, greedy):**
- mean decision points: **111.6** (limit [30, 120]);
- mean advance + compact status characters: **62,644** (limit 250,000);
- wall time: **0.76 s** in-process (max), **1.95 s** over MCP;
- max tool calls: **370 / 600**.
